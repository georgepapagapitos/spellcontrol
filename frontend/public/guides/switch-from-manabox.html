Switch from ManaBox to SpellControl

Scan or import your collection, and it files itself into your binders. ManaBox is good at getting cards into an app. SpellControl takes that same export and decides which binder and which page each card belongs on, using rules you set once.

Export from ManaBox

  1. In ManaBox, open your collection, or a single binder if you only want part of it.
  2. Choose Export and pick CSV.
  3. Get the file onto the device you will import from.

Import into SpellControl

  1. Open SpellControl. The add-cards panel opens.
  2. Drop the file in, or paste its contents. The format is detected for you.
  3. Review the resolved cards and confirm.

No account is needed. Your cards stay on your device until you sign in to sync them.

What carries over

  • Exact printing, from the Scryfall ID on every row.
  • Quantity and finish: nonfoil, foil or etched.
  • Condition and language for each copy.
  • Purchase price, kept on the card.
  • Altered and misprint flags.
  • ManaBox binder name, kept as each card's source category.

ManaBox binders are not recreated one for one. SpellControl binders are rules (color, type, set, price, tags), so there is nothing to rebuild by hand.

Your cards file themselves

Each card goes into the first binder whose rule matches it, and the binder shows the page and pocket it belongs in. Scan or import more cards later and they file themselves too. How rule-based binders work.

What changes

  • It runs in a browser. Phone, tablet or desktop, with nothing to install.
  • Sync is free. Sign in to keep your collection on every device. ManaBox's cloud sync guide lists sync as a Pro subscription feature, up to five devices, and says it is not visible on the ManaBox web version. Check ManaBox for its current terms.
  • Scanning works on mobile web. Scanned cards land in a list and are routed to a binder when you add them.
  • Free, no ads, no third-party trackers.

Notes

  • CSV and tab-delimited exports both work.
  • Importing twice adds copies. Delete an import from the import history to undo it.
  • For field-by-field detail, see the ManaBox import guide.
