Moxfield vs Archidekt vs ManaBox

Moxfield and Archidekt are websites for building decks. ManaBox is a phone app for scanning and tracking the cards you own. If you are choosing one, the first question is whether your time goes into building decks or into cataloging a physical collection. Most players end up with one of each kind.

Moxfield vs Archidekt

Both are free websites with public deck libraries, Commander support and price links. Reviewers tend to rate Moxfield higher for speed and a cleaner editor, and describe Archidekt as more visual and more customizable, with a steeper learning curve. Archidekt sorts cards into categories you name, such as Ramp or Removal, and shows them as card art. If you think in piles of art, try Archidekt. If you think in lists, try Moxfield. Decklists move between them as plain text.

Archidekt vs ManaBox

These barely compete. Archidekt is where you design a deck on a large screen; ManaBox is where you record which cards you physically own, using a camera. The overlap is collection tracking: Archidekt has one, with a CSV importer that offers presets for popular sites, but it has no scanner. ManaBox can import deck links from Archidekt, so a deck designed there can be loaded into the app to check against what you own.

Which should you start with?

  • A pile of paper cards and a phone: ManaBox.
  • A deck idea and a keyboard, sharing it with friends: Moxfield.
  • You like arranging decks visually: Archidekt.
  • All of the above: scan with ManaBox, build with Moxfield or Archidekt. CSV and text lists carry the data across.

Common questions

Which has the best collection tracker?

ManaBox, if you add cards by scanning. Moxfield and Archidekt track collections too, mainly so a deck can show which cards you already own.

Are they all free?

Yes for the core features. Moxfield and Archidekt are supported by optional Patreon memberships; ManaBox has a paid PRO tier.

Can I move my collection from one to another?

Yes. All three read and write CSV. Check that the file keeps printing, finish and condition, since a name-only list loses them.
